A mail runtime is a mail server: it receives mail for your domains, sends your users' mail, and keeps their mailboxes. Other runtimes, the CLI and the daemon's alerts can send through it.

socketley create mail m -p 25 --dns ns --no-reply -s            # only sends: alerts, notices
socketley send m --to ops@example.com --subject "Backup done" --text ok

socketley create mail m -p 25 --dns ns --idp auth --storage files/mail \
    --submission 587 --imap 143 --tls -s

What it does

Receiving

SMTP with SPF, DKIM, DMARC and ARC checks, optional greylisting and blocklists, and Sieve filters per user.

Safe spool

A message is synced to disk before the sender is told 250.

Sending

--submission and --submissions ports, DKIM signing, DANE and MTA-STS, or a --relay smarthost.

Reading

IMAP, POP3, JMAP, and webmail on --web.

DNS records

Publishes MX, SPF, DKIM, DMARC, MTA-STS and more through --dns.

Operating it

socketley mail m queue, show, flush, trace; on_rcpt and on_message in Lua.

Works with

  • dns publishes its domains' records.
  • idp provides its users.
  • storage holds the mailboxes.
