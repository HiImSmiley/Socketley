A mail runtime is a mail server. It takes the mail of its domains and the mail its users send, keeps each message in a spool synced to disk before it says 250, hands mail for elsewhere to the domain's exchanger or a smarthost, and keeps its users' mailboxes in a storage runtime, read over IMAP, POP3, JMAP or in the browser. A dns runtime gives its domains their records, an idp runtime its users, and every other runtime, the CLI and the daemon's alerts can send through it.

socketley create mail m -p 25 --dns ns --no-reply -s            # only sends: alerts, notices
socketley send m --to ops@example.com --subject "Backup done" --text ok

socketley create mail m -p 25 --dns ns --idp auth --storage files/mail \
    --submission 587 --imap 143 --tls -s

What it does

  • Receiving: SMTP on 25 with SPF, DKIM, DMARC and ARC checked, --greylist, DNS block lists, --clamav and --rspamd when given, --refuse-attachments, and Sieve filters per user (ManageSieve on --sieve).
  • Sending: --submission (STARTTLS) and --submissions (TLS) for users, DKIM signing with key rotation (--dkim-selector, --dkim-next), delivery over DANE and MTA-STS, retries until --queue-lifetime, or a --relay smarthost.
  • Reading: --imap/--imaps, --pop3/--pop3s, --jmap, and webmail on --web; quotas with --default-quota.
  • The domain's records: through --dns it publishes MX, SPF, DKIM, DMARC (--dmarc-policy, --dmarc-rua), MTA-STS, TLS-RPT and BIMI, and with --reports reads the reports others send back.
  • Operating it: socketley mail queue|show|flush|drop|trace|usage, suppression lists, and on_rcpt/on_message in Lua.
