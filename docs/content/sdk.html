The C++ SDK in include/linux/socketley/ comes in three tiers. Tier 1 is header-only and controls a running daemon over its socket. Tier 2 embeds the io_uring engine in your own binary: servers, clients, proxies and caches without a daemon. Tier 3 is header-only again and registers your own process as a runtime of a running daemon, so it appears in ls, is stopped with stop and can talk to the other runtimes. A cross-platform subset builds on Windows and macOS. Compiling examples are in sdk/examples; the test suite builds every one of them.
